ACCESS LEVELS

Choose the right level of access

Leadership access expands what someone can see and change. Review the responsibilities below before assigning a role.

RL
ONE REGION

Regional leader

Supports participants and ministry activity within one assigned Open Bible region.

  • View and manage people in their region
  • Review applications, assessments, reports, and pastoral references
  • Assign published pathway work, courses, events, and task plans
  • Create and manage events for their region
  • Add or remove regional leaders in the same region
Does not manage master course or task-plan content, the public website, other regions, or full administrators.
FA
ALL REGIONS

Full administrator

Has national access to participant work, leadership tools, and system-wide content.

  • Everything available to regional leaders across every region
  • Create, edit, publish, and assign courses
  • Create and manage master task-plan templates
  • Edit and publish the public CMC website
  • Assign or remove regional and full administrator access
Use this role sparingly. Full administrators can make changes that affect every region and the public website.
