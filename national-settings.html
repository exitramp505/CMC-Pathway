OFFICIAL MEETING CALENDAR

Google Calendar

Choose an organization-owned Google Calendar for CMC directors' meetings. All regional leaders can open this calendar from their dashboard. Only national administrators can change this connection.

Find this in Google Calendar → Settings for the dedicated calendar → Integrate calendar → Calendar ID. Share the calendar with authorized Google accounts. Do not paste a private iCal secret URL.

View calendar

This connects the calendar for viewing. Live server-side synchronization of private Google Calendar events requires separate authenticated Google API credentials; until enabled, CMC events and Google events remain distinct sources.
