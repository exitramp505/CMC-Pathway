OFFICIAL MEETING CALENDAR

Google Calendar

Choose an organization-owned Google Calendar for CMC directors' meetings. All regional leaders can open this calendar from their dashboard. Only national administrators can change this connection.

The Calendar ID identifies your organization calendar. It is not a secret. The event schedule appears in leaders' Pathway dashboards.

Keep the calendar automatically updated

For a private calendar, Google provides a Secret address in iCal format. Paste it here once to update CMC Pathway when events change, without making the calendar public.

Google Calendar → Calendar settings → Integrate calendar → Secret address in iCal format. This URL grants read access: keep it private. It is stored in a restricted server-side table and never returned to browsers.

View calendar

CMC Pathway uses Google Calendar as the schedule of record. Meeting notes, agendas, and follow-up tasks are managed separately inside CMC Pathway.
